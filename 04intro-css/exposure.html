Exposure Modes

Most cameras provide modes that determine how much control the photographer has.

Auto

In Auto mode (for idiots), the camera chooses aperture, shutter speed, and ISO. It is convenient for quick snapshots, but it may not produce the creative effect you want. Your smart phone uses this mode.

Aperture Priority

In Aperture Priority mode—often marked A or Av—you choose the aperture, and the camera chooses a matching shutter speed. This is useful when depth of field is your main concern.

Shutter Priority

In Shutter Priority mode—often marked S or Tv—you choose the shutter speed, and the camera chooses an aperture. This is useful when controlling motion is the priority.

Manual

In Manual mode (for real professionals), usually marked M, you choose both aperture and shutter speed, and often ISO as well. Manual mode gives full control and is especially useful when lighting is stable or when automatic metering is likely to be fooled.
